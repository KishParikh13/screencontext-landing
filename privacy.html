Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2, 2026

Our commitment: Your data belongs to you. We designed ScreenContext to be privacy-first from the ground up.

What We Collect

Screenshot Data:

  • All screenshots stored locally on your Mac
  • No screenshots uploaded to any server
  • No screenshots transmitted over the internet
  • You can delete your data at any time

Analytics (Optional):

  • Anonymous usage statistics (e.g., "app opened", "search performed")
  • No personally identifiable information
  • No screenshot content or OCR text
  • You can opt out in Settings at any time

What We DON'T Collect

  • ❌ We don't upload your screenshots
  • ❌ We don't read your OCR'd text
  • ❌ We don't track which apps you use
  • ❌ We don't share data with third parties
  • ❌ We don't train AI models on your data

Data Storage

All screenshot data is stored in ~/Documents/ScreenContext/ on your Mac. You can:

  • Browse these files directly in Finder
  • Back them up with Time Machine
  • Delete them manually
  • Move them to external storage

Permissions

ScreenContext requires:

  • Screen Recording permission - to capture screenshots
  • Accessibility permission - to detect active windows (optional)

These permissions are granted by you in macOS System Settings. We never access more than necessary.

Changes to This Policy

If we make changes to this privacy policy, we'll notify you via email (if you're on the waitlist) or in the app (if you're a user).
